# Spare

**A small, fast IPTV player for Android TV, built for finding a game and watching it.**

Bring your own playlist (M3U or Xtream Codes). Spare finds the live games, matches them to channels in your playlist, and plays the best stream with one press of OK.

Minimal by design: the whole app is under 2 MB and light on your TV's memory. Free, open source (MIT), no ads, no accounts, no tracking. If it's useful to you, you can [support it on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/mot1us).

## Why Spare

Most IPTV players are built around a channel list. When a game is on, you're left scrolling hundreds of channels and guessing which one has it. Spare started from the opposite question: *what's on right now, and where can I watch it?* Everything else stays small and quick enough for a budget streaming stick.

## What it does

**Sports**
- **Live now:** every live game from your leagues on one screen, plus live golf, tennis, racing and fights found in your guide.
- **One-press watching:** OK on a game plays the best matching stream. Hold OK for other streams.
- **Your leagues and teams:** NFL (with RedZone), MLB, NBA, NHL, Premier League, MLS, Champions League and college football, basketball and baseball. Reminders, spoiler-free scores, a score delay to match your stream, and replays where your provider keeps them.

**Live TV**
- A fast guide with categories, favorites, search (including voice) and catch-up.
- Frame-rate matching, audio tracks and subtitles.
- Multiview: two or four games at once.

**Movies and Series**
- Your provider's library, with ratings, Watchlist, Up next and Continue watching.

**Small and private**
- Under 2 MB, native Android TV app with no web views, so it stays quick on budget streaming sticks.
- Your logins and data stay encrypted on the TV, and updates install from inside the app.

Spare includes no channels or subscriptions.

## Get started

1. Download **spare.apk** from [Releases](https://github.com/mot1us/spare/releases) and install it on your Android TV (for example with the Downloader app).
2. Open Spare, go to **Settings → Playlists** and add your M3U link or Xtream login.
3. Open **Sports**, pick your leagues, and press OK on a game.

Spare updates itself: new versions appear under **Settings → Updates**.

## Remote basics

| Where | Button | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watching | Up / Down | Change channel |
| Watching | OK | Details, recent channels and actions |
| Watching | Left | Previous channel |
| Watching | Back | Open the guide |
| Sports | OK on a game | Play it |
| Sports | Hold OK | Choose a stream, set a reminder |
| Guide | Hold OK | Add or remove a favorite |
| Guide, Sports, Library | Left at the edge | Main menu (Search, Guide, Sports, Library, Settings) |
